# Relationship conversation evaluation

## Outcome and boundary

Create an IMStage project and a corresponding Eval Case Studio library of
natural synthetic WeChat/WhatsApp conversations. Exercise actual Talent Signal
contact, calendar, multimodal and public-person research paths, improve
confirmed failures, and verify the real product surface. The twelve public
authors are discussion subjects, never purported private correspondents.
Synthetic expectations are proposed; human labels remain pending. No external
invitation or calendar insertion is authorized by a model-generated draft.
