## Product invariant and scope

needs to see what is known, what changed, what needs judgment and one next step.

keep separate authority. Preserve identity, drafts, provenance, time, reversible
state and message-level authorization. Do not introduce person scores or widen
capture, model exposure, retention or external execution authority.

The shipped Mac client is `apps/macos`, a SwiftUI/AppKit/WebKit host. The
experimental `apps/macos-hybrid` shell is not substituted for its acceptance.
Scope includes the hosted Web, native window/chrome/menus, settings, appearance,
capture review and recovery. The existing quiet relational identity remains a
product constraint; structural alternatives must differ in reading order and
working mechanics, not just palette.
