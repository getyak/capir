# Rapid homepage reference set

Captured: 2026-08-05

This 20-minute sprint uses the existing repository research and the TOP1 seed
library rather than copying third-party pixels.

## Whole experience — Granola

- Source: https://www.granola.ai/
- Transfer: put high-fidelity product behavior close to the opening promise.
- Boundary: do not copy composition, wording, branding, or meeting-note visual language.

## Same-category semantics — Talent Signal product truth

- Source: `docs/product.md`
- Transfer: explain change, evidence, dependency, and the smallest safe action.
- Boundary: never imply autonomous outreach or candidate scoring.

## Typography and layout — existing Talent Signal system

- Source: `docs/design-system.md` and the incumbent homepage.
- Transfer: editorial hierarchy, warm neutral field, scarce vermilion, restrained materiality.
- Boundary: preserve the established type and token system.

## Interaction — existing LiveBrief

- Source: `apps/web/components/live-brief.tsx`
- Transfer: evidence toggles visibly alter interpretation and next action.
- Boundary: keep it an honest deterministic demonstration, not simulated automation.

## Anti-reference — generic AI command center

- Avoid glowing gradients, abstract intelligence graphs as primary proof, KPI tiles,
  candidate scores, and claims of invisible action.
