## Implementation and decisions

- Account/user-scoped, memory-only navigation retains exact destinations and
  visible contextual state; it does not store conversation text or evidence.
- Expanded/collapsed Session lists share Pin/Archive projection. Short-screen
  navigation scrolls while the account footer remains reachable.
- People search retains unsubmitted text, respects IME composition and uses
  exact Person IDs, including duplicate names and truncated long labels.
- Calendar retains day, view, timezone, filters and upcoming range. Shared reads
  coalesce. Account change, lowercase 401 and directory discard revoke data and
  fence pending responses; independent review closed the confirmed discard P1.
- Actual queued and legacy transcript regions restore bounded numeric reading
  offsets, with Session/account isolation and explicit user interruption.
  Framework auto-scroll is disabled on the actual runtime branches.
- On-demand Person background restores trigger focus on Escape/outside dismissal
  and leaves the conversation canvas width unchanged.
- Real browser acceptance exposed missing focus on new conversation. Commit
  `78fa83ba` carries explicit compose intent, validates the bootstrap Session and
  focuses the enabled ready composer once with `preventScroll`. Reopening a
  draft does not take focus; independent native review verified the delta.
- Final product review exposed the expanded header clipping New conversation.
  Its disclosure now occupies only remaining width; the visible action is 44px
  high. Person Preview also retains bounded, memory-only denial of discarded
  bindings across refresh, retry and reopening, without granting new authority.
