## Product and design invariant

The visitor is a person who wants a conversation to remain useful later, not
someone seeking a contact database administration task. The tension is forgetting
a promise, recognition clue, shared background, or change between exchanges.

The website must demonstrate this sequence using synthetic content:

1. share one chat screenshot;
2. see a useful, attributable understanding before long-term confirmation;
3. resolve material person ambiguity and review what matters;
4. keep a person/background/unfinished item in distinct governed views;
5. return or supply a later screenshot to update the same item.

Waiting, stopping, insufficient evidence and no-action are legitimate results.
Model interpretation never becomes confirmed fact or external action authority.
Existing purpose-bound internal intake persistence remains accurately described;
“review before durable confirmed context” must not falsely imply no temporary
processing or recoverable intake storage.

Five demonstration stories: first meeting and later retrieval, a small promise,
meeting preparation, a changed understanding, and choosing fewer reminders.
The initial hero explains one story clearly; other stories deepen it.
