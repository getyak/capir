## Design decision
Desktop knowledge workspace for relationship owners. Quiet neutral material,
readable 15px content, 24px headings, restrained press/focus feedback. Use page
and whitespace grouping. People prioritizes identity, context, and update;
Today prioritizes current action and ownership; technical provenance remains
inspectable through named details. Unavailable/partial source authority stays
visible. No decoration, invented score or new product workflow.
