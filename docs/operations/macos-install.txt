capri — personal Agent workspace

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

1. Drag the bundled app to Applications, then open it.
2. Paste the HTTPS workspace address from your workspace owner. Use the origin
   only (scheme, hostname and optional port), without /workspace or other paths.
3. Sign in with your own account.

The download includes no server, account, credentials, or private workspace
address. Installing it does not grant access to anyone else's data. A private
workspace requires an authorized Mac on the same Tailnet with Tailscale connected.

Use the network button in the workspace toolbar to change the server. Settings >
capri links to release downloads and installation help.

Signed releases check at launch and periodically while running. A verified update
appears beside the account avatar. Click Update and Restart once to download,
verify and restart; there is no second application confirmation. Settings >
Updates shows the installed version and check status. Offline checks retry later.
Unconfigured previews need one manual trusted installation to enable updates:
quit the app and replace it in Applications. Preferences and workspace accounts
are retained. Keep the prior download for operator rollback.

Preview packages are ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. Gatekeeper may block
them; do not disable Gatekeeper or strip quarantine. Use a signed, notarized
release for ordinary distribution.
