# Recruiting trend radar rubric

## Question quality

- **0:** Generic “what is trending?”
- **1:** Topic without a product decision.
- **2:** Decision exists but scope or horizon is vague.
- **3:** Clear capri decision, audience, market, and horizon.
- **4:** Also defines what evidence would change the decision.

## Source quality

- **0:** Unsourced or promotional aggregation.
- **1:** One vendor or influencer.
- **2:** Several sources of one type.
- **3:** Primary, empirical, practitioner, and counter-sources.
- **4:** Source diversity plus geographic/contextual relevance and dependency tracing.

## Signal classification

- **0:** Virality equals importance.
- **1:** Claims are listed without strength.
- **2:** Strength labels lack counterevidence.
- **3:** Evidence, independence, maturity, and counter-signals support classification.
- **4:** Classification is calibrated against prior signals and later outcomes.

## Product translation

- **0:** Trend produces an immediate feature demand.
- **1:** Generic recommendation.
- **2:** Relevant implication without test.
- **3:** `watch/test/build/avoid/no_action` with rationale and owner.
- **4:** A cheap falsifiable experiment and revisit trigger are defined.

## Intellectual honesty

Score 0 for hidden uncertainty or copied consensus. Score 4 for clear claim/source boundaries, corrected prior beliefs, and strong counterarguments.
