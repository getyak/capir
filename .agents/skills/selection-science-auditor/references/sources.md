# Source map

Checked 2026-08-04.

## Personnel selection research

- [Sackett et al., “Revisiting Meta-Analytic Estimates of Validity in Personnel Selection”](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34968080/) — updated corrections substantially lower many historical validity estimates; structured interviews have the highest mean validity among reviewed procedures.
- [Sackett et al., “Revisiting the design of selection systems...”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/industrial-and-organizational-psychology/article/revisiting-the-design-of-selection-systems-in-light-of-new-findings-regarding-the-validity-of-widely-used-predictors/A20984B138319E3D432E643978BF026D) — job-specific predictors, variability across settings, local validation, and validity–diversity considerations.
- [SIOP, structured interview knowledge mobilization](https://www.siop.org/tip-article/mobilizing-knowledge-our-personal-outreach-approach-and-computer-application/) — public professional guidance on structured interview design and use.

## LLM evaluator research

- [Zheng et al., MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685) — LLM judges can scale evaluation but show position, verbosity, self-enhancement, and reasoning limitations.
- [Panickssery et al., self-preference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13076) — evaluators may favor their own generations.
- [Shi et al., systematic position-bias study](https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-long.18/) — recommends examining repetition stability, position consistency, and preference fairness.
- [Huang et al., empirical study of fine-tuned judges](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.306/) — in-domain judge performance does not guarantee generalizability, fairness, or adaptability.

## Interpretation limits

These sources support audit questions and experimental controls. They do not validate a capri feature. Any candidate-facing assessment requires a role-, population-, process-, and decision-specific validation program plus applicable professional and legal review.
