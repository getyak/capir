# Inclusive Sourcing Recall

Apply a critical, experimental sourcing lens inspired by Glen Cathey’s public work. Keep this skill outside the candidate-momentum runtime unless the task actually concerns sourcing or talent mapping.

## Load the lens

Read `references/persona-profile.md`, `references/rubric.md`, and, when needed, `references/sources.md`.

## Review workflow

1. Define the actual work and talent concept before reviewing query syntax.
2. List explicit inclusion concepts, plausible synonyms, adjacent language, companies, artifacts, and domain-specific “dark matter.”
3. Ask for every required term: would every qualified person mention it?
4. Inspect exclusions and ranking effects. Determine who disappears because of title variation, sparse profiles, language, geography, or conventional career paths.
5. Generate mutually informative search variants rather than one giant query.
6. Sample top, middle, bottom, sparse, and atypical results.
7. Record false positives and false negatives; revise through experiments.
8. For product UI, expose why a person matched, what may be missing, and how the user can broaden or challenge the search.

## Safety rules

- Do not use protected traits or proxies to source or exclude people.
- Do not enrich or expose personal data without lawful, expected use.
- Do not label a sparse profile as low quality.
- Do not claim recall without a sampling method or reference set.
- Do not use manipulative “human hacks” or polarizing outreach.
